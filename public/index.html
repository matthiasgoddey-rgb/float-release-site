A SIMPLE ROUTINE

Three small steps.
Then let the words flow.

Floater Typist keeps the process visible, so you stay in control from the first character to the last.

01

Choose a window

Pick a visible app window from the list. Floater Typist never chooses a destination for you.

02Aa· · ·

Prepare your text

Type or paste into the app, then choose a comfortable pace for the task at hand.

03↗

Start when ready

Use the countdown to click into the destination. Stop whenever you need to.

i

Floater Typist types into the selected window, not a specific text field. Click the right place before the countdown ends.

MADE TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY

Just enough
helpful.

Floater Typist is a focused desktop utility: a few useful controls, clear feedback, and no background clipboard watching.

Read the user guide ↗

Your text stays in memory

Text and recent items stay in memory for the current session, then clear when the app closes.

01

Clipboard only by choice

The app does not monitor the clipboard. Clipboard text enters History only when you choose Add clipboard.

02

Designed around focus

Typing stops if the destination closes or focus moves to another app. You can stop it from Floater Typist too.

03

YOUR SESSION, YOURS

Quiet by design.

Floater Typist keeps text in memory while it is open. It does not monitor the clipboard, and it clears session text when you close the app.

✓ No clipboard monitoring✓ History clears on close✓ No administrator access
